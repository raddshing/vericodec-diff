- `src/rd_lora/training/timestep_routing.py` now derives routes from manifest timestep-band names and the live cell schema, and resolves the active `adapter_name` for an exact step index or timestep value.
- `scripts/train_rdlora.py` now validates the manifest through the runtime loader, checks backend alignment, writes `resolved_config.yaml`, materializes `adapter_plan.json` and `train_summary.json`, prints a concise plan summary, and exits without launching training.
